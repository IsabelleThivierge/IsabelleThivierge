Isabelle Thivierge

Founder and Director, DUJ-FEITH Foundation and DUJ-FEITH Labs Inc.

I work at the intersection of governed AI execution, agent safety, edge validation, and commercial deployment.

My research centres on a practical question:

When an AI system proposes an action, what determines whether that action should be allowed to become real?

I design and test policy gates, evaluation harnesses, simulations, multi-agent experiments, and vision-to-action pipelines—including physical-sensor validation on NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano.

Current work

* Governed execution for agentic and embodied AI systems
* Policy checks before real-world action
* Multi-agent stability and bounded system behaviour
* Edge-AI validation on constrained hardware
* Evaluation harnesses and reproducible experiments
* Governance and licensing structures for independent AI research
